How this works — five real, publicly-archived satellite/spacecraft IQ recordings (BY70‑2, NOAA‑15, CELESTA, FENGYUN‑4A, UHF‑FO — sourced from Zenodo and vksdr.com) were reduced to three physical-layer features each, after excluding two candidate recordings found to be calibration-tone artefacts rather than genuine signals. An Isolation Forest trained on ten windows of BY70‑2 correctly classified 4 of 5 satellites (80% accuracy) using no GPU. NOAA‑15 is honestly misclassified as genuine — its low phase-noise profile resembles BY70‑2's, a real, reported limitation rather than a hidden one. This console replays those real feature values, with small simulated jitter, as a continuous capture stream.
